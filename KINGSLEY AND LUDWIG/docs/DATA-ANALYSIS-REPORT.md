## 2. Geographic and respondent context

The 30 records cover 12 European countries: Finland 12 (40.0%); Sweden 3 (10.0%); Germany, Spain, France, Netherlands and Poland 2 each (6.7% each); Belgium, Denmark, Italy, Norway and the United Kingdom 1 each (3.3% each). ## 10. Interpretation limits

The study is purposive, non-probability, cross-sectional and based on self-reported assessments of one activity per respondent. Subgroups are small. The dataset does not verify 30 unique organisations, objective incident rates, financial accounts, detection times or recovery durations. Branch-specific governance variables are not identical constructs. Rating items are ordinal; means are descriptive summaries shown with medians. No inferential tests, causal estimates, population-representative conclusions or qualitative themes are reported.
