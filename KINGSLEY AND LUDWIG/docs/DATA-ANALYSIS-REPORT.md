## 9. Interpretation limits

Purposive, non-probability sample; self-reported and cross-sectional responses; small subgroups; activity-specific unit of assessment; no verified unique organisation key; no inferential testing. Do not claim representativeness, causation, statistical significance, or objective incident rates. All additional-insight source values are N/A, so qualitative themes and respondent quotations are unsupported.

## 10. Descriptive synthesis

The in-house group reported high internal skill dependency and high impact from internal resource disruption. The outsourced group reported high supplier dependency and supplier disruption impact, alongside a lower mean for monitoring effectiveness. The hybrid group reported comparatively modest ratings for responsibility clarity and cross-boundary information, and all nine selected coordination failure as their primary hybrid risk. Across the sample, operational disruption and customer/reputation damage were the most selected failure consequences. Cost/financial considerations and quality/performance were the most selected make-or-buy factors. These are sample-specific descriptive patterns, not causal findings.
